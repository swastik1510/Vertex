TIMING & HORIZON

Why 63 Trading Days (~3 Months)?

Day trading is noisy and costly, while 5-year investing is too slow. Here is why a 3-month positional window is the strategic sweet spot:

1 TO 5 DAYS

Day Trading & Short-Term F&O

  • Too much random noise: Short-term price swings are erratic, unpredictable, and stressful.
  • Fees and taxes eat profits: High brokerage charges, government taxes (STT), and slippage silently drain capital.
  • High failure rate: Official data shows over 90% of retail day traders lose money consistently.
63 TRADING DAYS (~3 MONTHS)

The Vertex Sweet Spot

  • Matches real money cycles: Aligned with company quarterly earnings reports and big institutional fund rebalancing.
  • Gives trends time to mature: Quality stocks have enough runway to trend upward without getting cut off by 1-day dips.
  • Low stress & low fees: Hold for ~1 quarter with minimal transaction costs and zero need to stare at screen charts all day.
3 TO 5 YEARS

Multi-Year Buy & Hold

  • Money locked up too long: Waiting years for an undervalued company to move ties up cash that could grow elsewhere.
  • Risk of holding losers: Many "cheap" stocks turn into value traps that underperform for years.
  • Misses fast rotations: Cannot capitalize on high-momentum sector rallies happening right now.
